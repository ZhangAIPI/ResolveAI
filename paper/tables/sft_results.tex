\paragraph{Source-supervised acquisition training.}
We adapt Qwen3-VL-8B with rank-32 LoRA on 1,280 CO3D development families, using 320 category-disjoint families for validation. Same-sequence views and cross-category negatives provide weak identity supervision. Expert calls execute in the environment, so the training history contains the photographs actually released and the failures actually returned. Training runs for 200 optimizer updates; the checkpoint is selected by held-out assistant-token loss. Table~\ref{tab:sft} compares this adapter with its frozen base on the same evaluation families. Agent label agreement falls from 82.3\% to 78.5\%: identity agreement rises from 91.1\% to 98.2\%, but condition agreement falls from 79.1\% to 71.2\%. The identity-only training therefore does not transfer uniformly across verification tasks. This pilot tests learning the acquisition and decision workflow; source-derived targets and simple request sequences do not establish a gain in reviewed evidence sufficiency or adaptive view selection.
