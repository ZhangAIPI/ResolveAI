MVTec AD~\cite{mvtec} &
Industrial anomaly detection and localization. &
Supplies condition photographs and anomaly annotations. We construct defect claims and vary access to the original image; different specimens are not treated as before--after pairs.\\
CO3D~\cite{co3d} &
Multi-view object reconstruction and novel-view synthesis. &
Supplies recorded object views and sequence metadata. We construct cross-photograph identity queries and requestable views; sequence membership remains a source-derived label.\\
BLINK / \mbox{MMPerspective}~\cite{blink,mmperspective} &
Visual perception questions; perspective perception, reasoning and robustness. &
Image reuse and controlled variants are established practices. Our versions change access to an evidence pool for an object claim, with requests and recorded outcomes.\\
ActiView / \mbox{ActiveVision}~\cite{actiview,activevision} &
Restricted-field zoom and shift; repeated scanning, traversal and attribute comparison. &
Observation choice is shared. Our requests release recorded photographs or return access failures while preserving the claim and object state.\\
WebQA / \mbox{AVerImaTeC}~\cite{webqa,averimatec} &
Multimodal source selection; image--text fact-checking with annotated web evidence and sufficiency checks. &
Evidence selection and sufficiency are shared concerns. We focus on photographed objects and paired changes in the availability of relevant records.\\
OpenEQA / \mbox{AbstainEQA}~\cite{openeqa,abstaineqa} &
Embodied questions with memory or exploration; original questions paired with abstention variants. &
Exploration and abstention are shared concerns. Our matched versions keep the object claim fixed while changing which photographs can be supplied.\\
CounterCredit~\cite{countercredit} &
A training method measuring whether visual calls were needed and their returned pixels were used. &
Shared-history action comparisons and content substitutions are prior methods. Our benchmark provides object-claim settings in which requests can succeed or fail.\\
